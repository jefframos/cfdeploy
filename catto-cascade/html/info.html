How to Play

The Catto above the golden paw is the one you are about to drop. You decide which side of the screen they go on by tapping.
When two Cattos touch they form a bigger Catto. Keep merging and see how big you can go!
Plan your merges by keeping an eye on which Catto is up next.
This is your total number of paw-points.
Click this to activate autoplay and fast track to the end of the game.

Powerups

Drop catnip to make the cats go wild and SHUFFLE around.
Drop a fish on a Catto to make it GROW one size larger.
Use the bottle to SPLIT up a big Catto into smaller ones!

Treasure Chests

Your chances of a reward increase based on the colored chest you unlock.
With every merge you gain paw-points.
Blue, red and pink paw-points will tell you which chest you are filling up.
Fill up the bar, by gaining the number of paw-points listed underneath, to unlock the chests.

Rewards

Play amount is selected before the game begins but is not spent until the end of the game.
Rewards are calculated as a multiplier of your play amount.
