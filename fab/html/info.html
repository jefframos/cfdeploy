How to Play

The horse cannon automatically rotates up and down
When you press the “shoot” button, the cannon will stop rotating then shoot the horse
You cannot control the horse while it is in the air
If the horse collides with obstacles in the air, they will be launched
The game is over once the horse has stopped moving

Prize Wheel

Select the play amount before launching the horse
The distance bar shows how far the horse has horizontally traveled. At certain thresholds the prize wheel will be upgraded
Once the horse lands, the prize wheel will open and automatically roll a multiplier for your play amount

Rewards

Rewards are calculated as a multiplier of your play amount
