Gameplay

Sink all the pirate ships by shooting squares on the board with the cannon. Try to get as few misses as possible!
The numbers at the top and sides tell you how many ships are in that column/row
The numbers on the board tell you how many Pirate Ships are next to them
Uncover more treasure islands for bigger rewards at the end of the game

Powerups

Mark a tile as safe so you don't accidentally shoot it
The barrage blows up a line of 5 tiles horizontally and vertically in a + shape. Tiles revealed without ships do not count as misses
The spyglass reveals 5 random tiles without ships
Wind will blow away all the clouds in a 3x3 area. Tiles revealed without ships do not count as misses

Rewards

Rewards are calculated as a multiplier of your play amount, selected before the game begins. The chance of receiving each reward, and the corresponding multiplier for that reward, are listed below:

When you hit play your reward is calculated, this is not linked to your performance throughout the game.
The number of treasure islands represents the size of your reward. There is no way you can miss out on collecting your reward and the round will not end until all treasure islands are found.
Auto play will generate a randomized play pattern to clear the board and take you straight to the rewards screen.
