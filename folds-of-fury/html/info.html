How to Play

Move the paper plane around the board to aim your weapon at enemies.
Enemies have different behaviors, memorize these patterns to predict where you will be safe.
Basic enemies drop blue crystals, collect these to unlock powerups.
Elite enemies have a psychedelic aura and are stronger than regular enemies.
Once defeated Elites drop a shiny purple token. Collect these before they slide off the board to earn a reward!
Turn autoplay on and off to fast track to the end of the game.
Watch out for your health!
If this runs out, so do your chances of a reward...
The Octoboss is your last adversary.
Dodge the projectiles while using your weapons.
Octoboss will lose limbs as their health decreases.

Experience Cards

Fill your experience to unlock powerups.
Choose Papercut to increase your shooting speed and damage.
Use Paperwaves to deal damage to all enemies in its path.
Increase close combat defense with rotating Blade Shields.
Harness the Wild Arrows, to shoot enemies in all directions.
Call a Crane Storm to deal major damage to your enemies.
Collect and stack weapons in preparation for the final battle.
Card numbers indicate the upgrade level for already unlocked powerups.
Invest in some health along the way!

Tokens

You can collect up to four tokens.
Each token represents one chance at a reward.
See the number of tokens collected at the top of the screen.
Rewards for any collected tokens will be shown at the end of the game.
If no tokens are collected play again for another chance at a reward.
Play amount per token is selected before the game begins...
...and shows max amount if all four tokens are collected.
See individual returns, along with total reward, at the end.
If no tokens are collected, play amount is not spent.

OctoBoss

You are given a unique screen to place a play amount before fighting the Octoboss.
If you fail to defeat the Octoboss, the play amount will remain between sessions.
Upon defeating the Octoboss, there is a special reward screen before the regular rewards.

Rewards
