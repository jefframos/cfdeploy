How to Play

The ball above the red arrow is the one you are about to drop. You decide which side of the screen they go on by tapping.
When two balls touch they form a bigger sports ball. Keep merging and see how big you can go!
Plan your merges by keeping an eye on which ball is up next.
Your score increases with every merge, this shows your total number of points!
Click this to activate autoplay and fast track to the end of the game.

Powerups

Drop this powerup to make the balls go wild and SHUFFLE around.
Drop this powerup to make any ball GROW one size larger.
Drop this powerup to SPLIT a big ball into smaller ones!

Treasure Chests

Your chances of a reward increase based on the colored chest you unlock.
Colored orbs are released with every merge.
Bronze, silver and gold colors will tell you which chest you are filling up.
Fill up the bar, by gaining the number of points listed underneath, to unlock the chests.

Rewards

Play amount is selected before the game begins but is not spent until the end of the game.
Rewards are calculated as a multiplier of your play amount.
