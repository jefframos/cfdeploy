Game Explanation

Every game generates a new board
The board is 5x5 consisting of a variety of tiles:
Starting Tile - Randomised point where the player begins
Pitfall Tile - Impassible tiles, randomly around the board
Regular Tile - Does nothing. Becomes a highlighted tile when next to a Gem Tile or Starting Tile
Highlighted Tile - Can be tapped to reveal either a Gem Tile or a Bomb Tile
Gem Tile - Shows one of three gems. When revealed, the player’s payout multiplier increases by one stage
Bomb Tile - When revealed, the player loses the game
Gold Tile - One per board. When tapped, the Golden Tile Minigame screen is opened
The Multiplier Track shows the currently earned multiplier, and that the maximum multiplier is x1000
The settle button allows you to end the game with the current multiplier, leaving the ‘Win Amount’ shown on the left

How To Play

At the start of each game, you must choose your play amount and currency
Then press continue
A random board is generated
You may then select a highlighted tile to reveal
The tile will then be revealed as a bomb, or a gem
If a bomb is selected, then the game ends, and you lose
If a gem is revealed then your multiplier increases
As well as your potential reward
The Settle button is revealed, and the game continues
The game then continues until either a bomb is struck, a bomb is reached, or you achieve the maximum multiplier
During the game, you can tap a golden tile. Doing so will prompt a second screen where you can set a play amount for the golden tile game, or leave
A table with three cups will appear, and shuffle around
You must pick a cup to either reveal:
A Big Win (Cup with lots of gems)
A Small Win (Cup with one gem)
A loss (Empty cup)
After the Golden Tile game is completed, the rewards are instantly won
The Golden Tile screen then closes, and the game continues as before

Rewards

Before each game, the player selects a play amount
This amount is held until the player settles or loses
Each time you select a non-gold tile, you’ll either be shown a gem or a bomb
If you get a bomb, then you lose your play amount and the game ends
If you get a gem, then your multiplier increases and the game continues
After earning one gem, you gain the option to settle before selecting each tile. Doing so will reward you with your play amount multiplied by your multiplier
This continues with increasing difficulty until you hit a bomb, settle, or reach the x1000 multiplier
During the game, you may reach the golden tile. Instead of a bomb or gem, you will unlock a minigame
To play the minigame, select a new play amount
The cups will then shuffle around, before stopping and allowing you to pick one. Your cup will either reveal a pile of gems, one gem, or nothing
You then immediately receive your reward, and the minigame closes
