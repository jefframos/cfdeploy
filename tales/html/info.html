Abilities

Every turn, the ability bar will fill by 1. When full, you can tap the heart to enter or exit healing mode.
In Healing Mode, the letters turn green. You can select 5 letters and heal that amount back. You also block the upcoming enemy attack.

Progression

After beating the third and fifth fight, you will earn a reward based on your play amount since the last chest.
If you are beaten or flee, your reward builds up till the next chest you open.
The play amount shows you how much you have currently put towards the next reward.
